# LangChain-AI-Fridge-Recipe-Generator
AI-powered LangChain application that analyzes fridge images, identifies ingredients, and generates personalized recipes using multimodal AI.

🧊 AI Fridge Recipe Generator

An AI-powered LangChain application that analyzes an image of your refrigerator, identifies available food ingredients, and generates personalized recipe recommendations based on what you already have.

The application combines multimodal AI, LangChain, LLMs, and image understanding to turn a simple fridge photo into practical meal ideas with step-by-step recipes.

🚀 How It Works
📸 Upload a fridge image — Provide an image containing the available ingredients.
👁️ Ingredient Detection — The multimodal AI model analyzes the image and identifies visible food items.
🧠 Recipe Generation — LangChain orchestrates the AI workflow to determine what can be prepared using the detected ingredients.
🍳 Personalized Recipes — The application generates suitable recipes with ingredients, quantities, preparation steps, and cooking instructions.
💡 Smart Suggestions — Recipes are prioritized based on the ingredients already available, helping reduce food waste and unnecessary grocery purchases.

🎯 Use Case

The goal is to build a practical AI cooking assistant that answers:

"I have these ingredients in my fridge — what can I cook with them?"

Instead of searching through hundreds of recipes manually, users can simply take a picture of their fridge and let AI generate relevant meal options.
